Naya Aasha has existed since 2016. This website has existed for about a week. That gap says something about how we have worked so far. Registering an organization, opening the doors, sitting with families  all of that came first. Telling people about it kept getting pushed down the list. So this is us catching up. Naya Aasha works with children whose schooling is interrupted by cost, with people living with HIV who face stigma far more often than they face a shortage of medicine, and with families in Lalitpur managing more than any family should have to manage alone. We are small. We work with what we have, and we are careful about what we promise. This site is where that work will be visible from now on. Program updates as they happen. Ways to help, if you want to. Honest accounting of what comes in and where it goes  including the times we fall short. You will notice some parts are still unfinished. Our donation account is being formally verified, and we would rather publish nothing than publish details we cannot yet stand behind. Those pages will fill in as things are confirmed, not before. If you want to volunteer, partner with us, or simply understand what we do, write to nayaaashanepal@gmail.com or call +977 9803095587. We read everything. Thank you for being here at the start of this part.
